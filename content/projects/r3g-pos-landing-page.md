## Overview

The R3G Software POS landing page is a live marketing website that introduces the R3G Software POS product to prospective customers — particularly small businesses and retail or F&B operators. The page presents the product's features, shows it in action, explains that it installs like an app and works on any screen, and invites owners to join as early testers.

The website is publicly accessible and released. It is the marketing site for the product, and is separate from the R3G POS application itself, which is a collaborative business venture rather than a single-owner product.

![R3G Software POS landing — feature overview section](/images/portfolio/r3g-pos-landing-page/02-overview.webp)

## My Role & Responsibilities

My individual contribution is the landing page: I designed it and built it as its web developer, then shipped it to a live, deployed site. That covered the page's narrative and layout, the responsive front-end implementation, and getting it to release.

This case study is about the landing page. It does not claim ownership of the R3G POS application's product or business logic — that is the shared venture the page promotes.

## The Challenge

When everything at a small business is done manually, operations get slow and error-prone — especially during a rush. The product addresses that, but the landing page has its own job: explain a POS clearly to a busy owner, show it working on both desktop and phone, and turn interest into an early-tester signup, without overstating what the product is.

## Design Process

I structured the page as a narrative: lead with the manual-work problem, answer it with a clear solution and a feature overview, show the product in action, explain that it installs like an app (PWA) and works on any screen, then invite owners into the early-tester program through a short, four-step path and a simple form, closing with an FAQ and a final call to action. I designed the desktop and mobile layouts together and implemented the whole page as responsive front-end code.

![How it works — the four-step early-tester onboarding](/images/portfolio/r3g-pos-landing-page/03-process.webp)

## Final Solution

The result is a live, responsive landing page: a hero that states the product in one line, a feature section covering the cashier, QR table ordering, dine-in and open bills, a kitchen display, stock tracking and sales reports, a visual product showcase, a PWA-install explanation, an early-tester program with a signup form, and an FAQ. The tone is calm and legible rather than loud, matching the product's "simple, no new complexity" promise.

![Product showcase — the POS in action, from choosing a menu to payment](/images/portfolio/r3g-pos-landing-page/04-solution.webp)

## Responsive Experience

The page was designed and built to work wherever it would actually be read — a full desktop layout for an owner's laptop and a tight mobile layout for a phone — from one responsive implementation.

![R3G landing page on desktop (1280) and mobile (390)](/images/portfolio/r3g-pos-landing-page/05-responsive.webp)

## Technology Stack

The site is built with Next.js, React and TypeScript, styled with Tailwind CSS, and uses Lucide React for icons. Nodemailer and Sharp are also part of the project's dependencies. I'm listing the stack as the technologies the project is built on, without attributing a specific finished feature to each package.
